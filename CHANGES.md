# Changes

## 1.8 (2026112700)

- Moodle 4.5 to 5.3; sites on 4.4 stay on 1.8 R2. (R3)
- Hero activities, the course link on activity pages and the tabs of Designer Pro work with the new course menu of
  Moodle 5.3, at the positions set for them. (R3)
- Mark as done works on Moodle 5.3. (R3)
- The colours follow the dark mode of Boost (Moodle 5.3, experimental). (R3)
- The Message and Add to contacts buttons of the course staff only appear when they work, e.g. not through guest
  access. (R3)
- Drag and drop of activities shows where the activity will go. (R3)
- With one section per page, the section cards of a row have the same height. (R3)
- In edit mode, the menus of an activity open above the next activities. (R3)
- New course header type "Basic layout" for Designer Pro, so courses keep the look of Designer Basic when Designer Pro
  is installed. (R3)
- An outdated Designer Pro (older than 1.7 R5) is switched off with a notice for admins instead of breaking Designer
  courses. (R2)
- Security: custom activity titles and profile fields are escaped; the Video Time web service, section backgrounds and
  the staff in the course header follow the user's access and privacy settings; the progress popover no longer lists
  hidden activities. (R2)
- Section pages use Moodle's section page, with a breadcrumb back to the course. (R2)
- Faster course pages: visit counts and Video Time records are loaded once per page. (R2)
- Activity descriptions keep their formatting and are trimmed by whole words. (R2)
- The course index updates again on Moodle 4.5, and Kanban columns show one card per row again. (R2)
- New setting Card width for the Cards and Image cards layouts: fill the row, or keep the column width.
- Moodle 4.4 to 5.2.
